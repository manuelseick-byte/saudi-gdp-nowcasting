# Data sources

The project uses monthly and quarterly macroeconomic indicators for Saudi Arabia covering January 2015 to June 2026.

## Variables

### Baseline specification

- Real GDP — quarterly target variable
- Foreign reserves — monthly
- Goods exports — monthly
- Goods imports — monthly
- Brent crude oil price — monthly
- Point-of-sale transactions — monthly

### Extension specification

The extension changes the target to real non-oil GDP and adds or substitutes indicators intended to capture non-oil activity and financial conditions:

- Real non-oil GDP — quarterly target variable
- Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI) — monthly
- Tadawul All Share Index (TASI) — monthly
- Financial balance of payments — quarterly

## Source institutions

The project paper identifies the following data sources:

- General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT)
- Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)
- IMF International Financial Statistics
- U.S. Energy Information Administration
- JODI Oil
- Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)

The underlying source files and project workbook are not redistributed in this public repository.
